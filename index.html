About the Infrastructure

This domain operates as the dedicated knowledge layer of the NVO987 network. It hosts structured publications, research resources, metadata systems, semantic schemas, archival records, and machine-readable knowledge assets.

The infrastructure is designed to ensure discoverability, interoperability, traceability, and long-term accessibility across multiple generations of web technologies.

Mission

The mission of the Knowledge Infrastructure is to preserve, organize, and distribute knowledge through open standards, semantic technologies, decentralized identity systems, and sustainable publication architectures.

Preservation

Long-Term Continuity

Knowledge should remain accessible beyond technological cycles, software platforms, and changing digital environments.
